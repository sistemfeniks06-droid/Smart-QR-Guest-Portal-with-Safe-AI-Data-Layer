# Smart QR Guest Portal with Safe AI Data Layer

A lightweight, mobile-first hospitality landing portal accessed via tabletop QR codes that bridges instant guest utilities with dual-channel sentiment feedback routing and AI-discoverable semantic data.

## The Unique Kernel
Separating physical QR printing from dynamic digital venue data, coupled with dual-channel sentiment feedback: positive ratings (4–5 stars) route directly to Google Reviews to build public reputation, while lower ratings (1–3 stars) route privately to management via WhatsApp for immediate resolution before public complaints occur.

## Who It's For
Restaurant, cafe, and boutique hotel guests seated at a table who want immediate access to Wi-Fi and digital menus without downloading mobile apps; and venue managers who need an automated way to protect and increase their Google rating.

## The Core Loop
The guest scans the tabletop QR code, instantly accesses Wi-Fi credentials and menu items on a mobile splash interface, and submits quick sentiment feedback before departure.

## Inspiration & Identity
- Aesthetic: Clean, tactile card-based hospitality UI with neutral tones.
- Performance: Ultra-fast loading (<500ms), zero trackers, zero app installs.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
Solves a direct business problem for hospitality venues: avoiding costly reprint cycles for QR materials while protecting online reputation and structuring data for AI search engines.

## What "Working" Looks Like
A guest scans the QR, connects to Wi-Fi with one tap, views categorized menu items, and tests feedback routing: high ratings open Google Reviews, lower ratings open WhatsApp.

## The POC Boundary
- Single responsive web portal.
- Instant Wi-Fi copy/connect button.
- Clean categorized digital menu modal.
- Dual-channel review routing logic.
- Embedded Schema.org / JSON-LD metadata layer.

## Later
- Multi-language localization switcher.
- Venue manager admin panel for dynamic menu updates.

## Explicitly Cut
- Heavy frontend frameworks (React/Vue) cut in favor of pure lightweight HTML/CSS/JS.
- Native mobile application cut to eliminate guest friction.
